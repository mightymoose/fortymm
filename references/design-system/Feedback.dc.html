Containers, feedback, overlays

Each role has its own structure: card for persistent info, notice for a sentence in the flow, alert for a problem with a fix, toast for brief success, tooltip for supplementary text. Overlays are drawn in place (static) so they screenshot without opening.

--bg-panel, no border. Title and status top-right, label/value details, actions bottom-left with the primary first.

Warning and error

Tinted fill and full hue edge. Title is the problem, body is why, buttons are the fix.
