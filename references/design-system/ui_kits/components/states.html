Disabled ignores the pointer

Disabled buttons, fields and controls look identical on hover and press. Say why nearby in help text — a tooltip can't be reached on a disabled control.

Loading stays readable

A loading button keeps its colours, swaps the label to "Saving…" and shows a static ring. It ignores further clicks until done.

Focused errors stay errors

An invalid field keeps its 2px red edge, icon and message while focused. The orange focus ring is added outside, never replaces them.

Selected has a non-colour cue

Chips gain a check, tabs an underline, segmented options a filled weight, blocks an outline. State is exposed with aria-pressed, aria-checked or aria-selected.

Edges by role

Interactive edges use --border-field (3:1). Dividers use --border-subtle. A field is always brighter than the line under a row.

Focus is always the ring

Every focusable thing shows the same 2px orange ring with a dark gap. Tabs draw it inset so it isn't clipped by the scroll edge.
